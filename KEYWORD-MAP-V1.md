# Maalaus Multiväri — SEO Keyword Map v1

## Status
PLANNING / RESEARCH — not a claim of search volume.

## Rules
- No invented search volumes, CPC or difficulty scores.
- Validate numeric opportunity in Google Search Console and Keyword Planner before prioritising pages.
- Do not create thin city + service pages programmatically.
- A keyword maps to one primary canonical page; supporting pages can reinforce it with internal links.
- Location pages must contain genuinely local, useful information and only target areas the business can actually serve.

## Competitor observation
Current search results show competitors winning with explicit service + location pages and clear quote CTAs. Examples include Maalausliike ILME's Helsinki-specific interior-painting page, MainioMaalaus's Helsinki exterior-painting page, and Fiksumaalaus's Porvoo painting page. Cleaning competitors such as Sivex expose service-specific navigation and a fast quote flow. This supports our service + location + conversion architecture, but does not establish exact keyword volumes.
