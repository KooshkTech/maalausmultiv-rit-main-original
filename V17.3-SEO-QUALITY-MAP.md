## Intended hierarchy

`Homepage → Main service → Service × city → Local project proof / related local service → Quote`

Supporting content follows:

`Blog article → Main service → Relevant Helsinki/Espoo/Vantaa money page`

## Cannibalization guardrails

- City hubs target broad local company/service intent.
- Main service pages target the generic service across Uusimaa.
- Service × city pages target the specific service + city combination.
- Blog posts target informational questions and should support, not duplicate, commercial money-page intent.
- No additional service × city combinations should be created without evidence from Search Console or another reliable demand source.

## Automated gate

Run:

```bash
npm run verify:v17:quality
```

It checks:
- uniqueness of the 15 generated SEO titles and descriptions,
- sitemap coverage and duplicate paths,
- required parent/city/cross-service links,
- blog-to-local-page link support,
- supporting-content coverage,
- legacy 5-year warranty wording,
- duplicate literal SEO metadata warnings.
